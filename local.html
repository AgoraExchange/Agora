A few straight answers.

Why pay for this if Google gives me a free QR code?

If you only need a Google review link, Google's free QR may be enough. Agora builds a branded page combining your next steps, sets it up for you, and can maintain it as your business changes.

Does Agora Tap automate reviews?

Agora Tap gives customers an easy way to leave a review; it does not write or post reviews automatically. Want help reaching more potential customers? Agora Analysis is a separate software and managed promotion add-on for Agora Tap purchasers and clients on an existing Agora plan, at an additional $35/month. Agora handles targeted campaigns around your business and audience. Reviews must come from people with a genuine experience of your business. Review counts, star ratings, and rankings are not guaranteed. Explore Agora Analysis ↗

Is there a dashboard or automated texting?

Those are custom additions, not starter features. We can discuss lead capture, reporting, and notifications once we understand what you need.

Do I need to buy an NFC stand?

No. Start with the QR code. We can help connect a compatible card you already own or quote hardware separately.
